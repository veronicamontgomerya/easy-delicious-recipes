Instant Pot Chana Masala

I got this from the Indian Instant Pot cookbook, but I'm linking the corresponding recipes on the author's blog. You'll want the onion masala first — it's the base for a lot of what's below.

Base Onion masala ↗ Recipe Homestyle chana masala ↗
The hack

Use canned chickpeas to skip the long soak.

Recipe modifications
  • Substitute the 1 cup dry chickpeas with 2 cans, drained and rinsed.
  • Eliminate the 18-minute high-pressure cycle entirely — simmer on the stove or in Sauté mode for roughly 5 minutes, just to meld the flavors.
  • Reduce the 3 cups water down to 1–1.5 cups.
  • Mash roughly half the chickpeas into a paste to help thicken the sauce. Keep mashing at the end until it reaches the consistency you want.

Korean Tofu Stir Fry

I love this recipe, but I also like to make a lot at a time — which meant spending almost an hour frying tofu in separate batches. Baking it on sheet pans let me condense down to a single batch, prep the sauce and vegetables concurrently, and get the tofu texture consistent.

Recipe Kkampoong tofu ↗
The hack

Bake the tofu in the oven while you prepare the sauce and vegetables at the same time.

Recipe modifications
  • Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  • After coating the tofu, spread it on a lined baking sheet in a single layer and bake 30–35 minutes.
  • About 20 minutes in, start preparing the sauce and vegetables.
  • Follow the end of the recipe: add the sauce to the cooked vegetables, then slide the baked tofu in.

Recipe Dal (Indian lentil curry) ↗
The hack

Use canned or pre-steamed lentils instead of dry.

Recipe modifications
  • Substitute 2 cans of drained and rinsed lentils, or 1 lb pre-steamed, for the 1 cup dry.
  • Eliminate the 1-hour soak and the 90-minute simmer — active cooking drops to 15–20 minutes.
  • Reduce the water from 4 cups to about 1.5 cups.
  • Use an immersion blender at the end to get the creamy texture.

Elote Bowls

My boyfriend and I made this up after discovering the Everything but the Elote seasoning from Trader Joe's. It's become a weeknight regular. Enjoy!

Key ingredient Everything but the Elote seasoning ↗

Servings: 4

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • — Everything but the Elote seasoning
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 2 jalapeños, chopped
  • 1 shallot, diced
  • — garlic salt, to taste
  • — taco seasoning, to taste
  • 2 cans corn
  • 1 cup Greek yogurt
  • — Mexican-style shredded cheese, a handful
  • 2 avocados

Instructions

  1. Heat the olive oil over medium heat and add the jalapeños and shallots. Season with garlic salt. Stir until they're starting to look softer.
  2. Add the ground turkey and season heavily with garlic salt and taco seasoning. Break up the turkey and stir it in with the jalapeños and shallots. Cook about 10 minutes, until the turkey is browned and starting to get crispy.
  3. While the turkey and vegetables cook, drain the corn and combine it with the Greek yogurt in a large bowl. Drench with Elote seasoning. Add the cheese and stir everything together, then add some more Elote seasoning.
  4. When the turkey is done, add it to the bowl with the corn. Stir everything together and serve over rice. Top with avocado sprinkled with Elote seasoning.
